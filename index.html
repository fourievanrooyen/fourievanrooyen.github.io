About Me

I am a senior Aerospace Engineering student at the University of Alabama with a strong focus on applied aerodynamics, propulsion, and experimental validation. My experience centers on CAD-driven design and high-fidelity analysis using CFD, FEA, and multiphysics tools—primarily ANSYS and SolidWorks—supported by MATLAB and Python for performance evaluation, data processing, and system-level modeling. I work best in hands-on, multidisciplinary environments where analytical predictions are validated through testing, iteration, and real-world performance, and I am motivated by closing the gap between simulation and physical behavior.

Projects

Capstone Project • Chief Engineer

eVTOL Reconnaissance UAV

ORCA is a fixed-wing VTOL unmanned aircraft designed to combine vertical takeoff and landing capability with efficient forward flight using a tilting-propulsion configuration. The platform emphasizes low-Reynolds-number aerodynamic efficiency, lightweight additive manufacturing, and thermal-resilient materials to support reliable flight operations.

  • Led system-level design of a fixed-wing eVTOL reconnaissance UAV, integrating distributed electric propulsion, solar film wing augmentation, and a modular payload architecture within FAA Part 107 constraints.
  • Defined mission requirements, Level I/II technical specifications, and Concept of Operations (ConOps) following NASA NPR 7123.1D and AIAA systems engineering practices.
  • Developed and refined the 3D airframe geometry, including winglet and nose modifications, to improve aerodynamic efficiency, stability, and manufacturability.
  • Executed full aerodynamic, structural, and thermal analyses in ANSYS, including CFD, FEA, and two-way fluid structure interaction (FSI), validating structural deflections below 1 mm under flight loads.
  • Conducted experimental crush testing on 3D-printed structures to evaluate infill strategies, identifying 7% gyroid infill as the optimal strength-to-weight configuration and incorporating results into structural sizing.
  • Oversaw cross-subsystem integration (aerodynamics, structures, propulsion, avionics, and manufacturing), resolving CG alignment, power, and mass tradeoffs to support Preliminary Design Review readiness.
