About

Welcome to my portfolio — I'm a senior Aerospace Engineering student with a focus on applied aerodynamics, propulsion systems, and experimental validation. I use CAD, CFD, FEA, and multiphysics analysis in ANSYS and SolidWorks, alongside MATLAB and Python, to evaluate aerodynamic performance and structural response under realistic conditions. I work best in hands-on environments that demand both analytical rigor and practical execution.
