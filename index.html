Senior Aerospace Engineering Student

I’m Fourie van Rooyen, a senior Aerospace Engineering student with a strong emphasis on applied aerodynamics, propulsion, and experimental validation. My technical interests lie in using CAD, CFD, FEA, and multiphysics analysis, primarily in ANSYS and SolidWorks, alongside MATLAB and Python to evaluate aerodynamic performance, structural response, and system behavior under realistic operating conditions. I’m particularly drawn to problems where simulation informs hardware decisions and where results can be verified through testing and iteration.

I work best in hands-on, multidisciplinary environments that demand both analytical rigor and practical execution. Whether refining aerodynamic models, interpreting test data, or supporting experimental setups, I’m motivated by closing the loop between analysis and real-world performance. Outside of engineering, I enjoy staying active and balanced, watching and following hockey and rugby, spending time outdoors, hiking, and exploring new places whenever possible.
