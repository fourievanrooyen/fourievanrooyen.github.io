Genesis Drone Simulation Project

  • Engineered a sophisticated quadcopter simulation in Python leveraging the Genesis physics engine, incorporating real‑time aerodynamics, rigid‑body dynamics, and control systems.
  • Integrated PID controllers for altitude hold, attitude stabilization (roll, pitch, yaw) and position maintenance, tuned with gains derived from engineering principles to achieve critical damping (ζ ≈ 0.7–1.0) and sub‑second settling times, preventing oscillations and flips.
  • Implemented manual flight controls via Pygame for intuitive WASD/spacebar inputs, mapping to differential RPM adjustments on propellers, alongside automated modes for hover and leveling using quaternion‑to‑Euler conversions and vector rotations.
  • Developed multi‑environment support for parallel drone instances, benchmarked for performance (e.g., >1000 steps/sec on CUDA), with custom camera rendering (third‑person and minimap views) and HUD overlays displaying RPM, velocity, thrust, drag and Euler angles.
  • Optimized for realism by incorporating gravitational forces, empirical thrust/drag calculations and anti‑windup in PIDs, demonstrating proficiency in control theory, numerical methods and software.
