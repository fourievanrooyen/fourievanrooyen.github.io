Capstone Project – Chief Engineer

Tuscaloosa, AL (Aug 2025 – Present)

  • Led system-level design of a fixed-wing eVTOL reconnaissance UAV, integrating distributed electric propulsion, solar film wing augmentation, and a modular payload architecture within FAA Part 107 constraints.
  • Defined mission requirements, Level I/II technical specifications, and Concept of Operations (ConOps) following NASA NPR 7123.1D and AIAA systems engineering practices.
  • Developed and refined the 3D airframe geometry, including winglet and nose modifications, to improve aerodynamic efficiency, stability, and manufacturability.
  • Executed full aerodynamic, structural, and thermal analyses in ANSYS, including CFD, FEA, and two-way fluid structure interaction (FSI), validating structural deflections below 1 mm under flight loads.
  • Conducted experimental crush testing on 3D-printed structures to evaluate infill strategies, identifying 7% gyroid infill as the optimal strength-to-weight configuration and incorporating results into structural sizing.
  • Oversaw cross-subsystem integration (aerodynamics, structures, propulsion, avionics, and manufacturing), resolving CG alignment, power, and mass tradeoffs to support Preliminary Design Review readiness.

A.A.E.R.O. Drone Propeller Design

  • Lead design of drone propeller with vortex inducers to evaluate aerodynamic performance.
  • Simulated propeller thrust and acoustic characteristics using Computational Fluid dynamics (CFD) in ANSYS and SolidWorks and compared with real-world test data.
  • Performed Finite Element Analysis (FEA) on designs to assess structural integrity and material performance.
  • Applied Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T) for precise manufacturing of components.
  • Conducted static test stand experiments measuring thrust, RPM, and acoustics for multiple design iterations.
  • Utilized wind tunnel for aerodynamic data collection and model validation.

Alabama Rocketry Association – Liquids Team

  • Collaborated on designing components for a single-stage, liquid propellant rocket, focusing on fluids systems.
  • Designed test stand and created P&ID diagrams adhering to safety standards.
  • Performed hydrostatic proof testing of flight hardware to demonstrate tank compliance with MIL-STD-810G/SMC-S 016 qualification requirements.
  • Utilized a CNC Lathe to manufacture a graphite nozzle within design tolerances.
  • Assisted in setup and execution of multiple hot fire tests for LOX/Kerosene engine, including short-duration and duration tests at Tuscaloosa National Airport, analyzing performance data to identify areas for improvement.

Genesis Drone Simulation Project

  • Engineered a sophisticated quadcopter simulation in Python leveraging the Genesis physics engine, incorporating real-time aerodynamics, rigid-body dynamics, and control systems.
  • Integrated PID controllers for altitude hold, attitude stabilization (roll, pitch, yaw) and position maintenance, tuned with gains derived from engineering principles to achieve critical damping and sub-second settling times.
  • Implemented manual flight controls via Pygame for intuitive WASD/spacebar inputs, alongside automated modes for hover and leveling using quaternion-to-Euler conversions and vector rotations.
  • Developed multi-environment support for parallel drone instances, benchmarked for performance (>1000 steps/sec on CUDA), with custom camera rendering and HUD overlays displaying RPM, velocity, thrust, drag and Euler angles.
  • Optimized for realism by incorporating gravitational forces, empirical thrust/drag calculations and anti-windup in PIDs.
